The masking tax

The barrier isn't competence. It's translation.

The barrier is not competence — it is communication. Workplace norms are built around neurotypical defaults that are invisible to those who set them and exhausting for those who must decode them. The result is "masking": a hyper-monitoring state that enables job access but leads to burnout, often within months.

The product

Four layers of quiet help, woven into the apps you already use.

01 — The Interpreter

Decodes ambiguous messages in real time.

Plain-language readouts of what a manager's vague Slack message likely means, what action is expected, and by when. Live tone shifts surfaced during meetings.

02 — Instruction Decomposer

Turns vague asks into concrete steps.

Breaks "can you take a look at the deck and clean it up" into 3–5 specific, ordered actions with time estimates and clarifying questions to send back.

03 — Social Energy Tracker

Budget for masking, before burnout.

Models the cognitive cost of meetings, async load, and code-switching. Recommends recovery windows and flags overload before it lands.

04 — Culture Map

Makes implicit office rules explicit.

Learns your org's unwritten norms — who replies fast, what "let's circle back" means here, when 4pm Friday Slack is OK — and surfaces them on demand.

How it works

One install. Opt-in per person. Private by design.

01
Install where you work
A single admin install pushes the Cognize layer to Slack, Teams, Zoom, and Meet across your org. Zero per-seat configuration.
02
Opt-in, individually
Each employee chooses which features to turn on. Nothing is broadcast, nothing is logged to managers. Private by design.
03
Translation happens inline
Cognize annotates messages, transcribes meetings, and decomposes tasks within the apps your team already uses — no new dashboard to learn.
04
The org learns its own norms
Anonymized, aggregated patterns refine the Culture Map over time, making the workplace progressively more legible for everyone.
